About Me

I’m Obuo David James (popularly known as OGA DAVE), a passionate entrepreneur driven by innovation and impact. Through OGA DAVE CONCEPTS, I’ve built ventures across Technology, Cryptocurrency, and Real Estate — empowering individuals and businesses to grow in the modern market.
